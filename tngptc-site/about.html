About the college

A premier technical institute in Southern India, as described on the official website.

Illustration

Mission

Tamil Nadu Polytechnic College, Madurai empowers youth through industry-aligned diploma and skill programmes, fostering employability in urban and rural sectors, with flexible and inclusive education, career guidance and strong industry partnerships.

History

YearMilestone
1890Established by the District Board of Madurai; later merged with the Municipal Industrial School.
1910Administration taken over by the Madras Presidency.
1919Became the Government Industrial Training Institute, offering technical certificate courses.
1946Dravida Polytechnic established alongside the Industrial Training Institute under the Post-War Development Scheme; later renamed Tamil Nadu Polytechnic College, then Tamil Nadu Government Polytechnic College.
1957Came under the Director of Technical Education, Chennai, after the Department for Technical Education was created.

Source: Institute Profile, official homepage, checked 13 October 2026.
