Services

Beyond the classroom

  • Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Training Center
    Education and training on methods used to evaluate the properties of materials, components or structures without causing damage.
  • Canada India Institutional Cooperation Project (CIICP)
    Vocational courses in Fire & Safety, AC Servicing and Beautician training.
  • Material testing in Civil Engineering
    Tests on cement, sand, aggregate, water, concrete, brick, paver block, Tor steel and mild steel, to check compliance with relevant Indian standards.
